Corporate Fast · October 23–29

Leading Up to the Conference

We enter Catalyst together — a seven-day corporate fast from October 23 through the 29th, in preparation for what God wants to do among us. The fast concludes as we gather Thursday evening, breaking it together over dinner. Guidelines will be sent to every registrant ahead of time.

Ephesians 1:3–4

“Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us in Christ with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly places, even as he chose us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and blameless before him.”

Four days to remember who you are in Him — chosen, adopted, and sent out empowered.

Families

Childcare

We want both parents fully present in every session. A staffed children’s program — ages 4–12, potty-trained — runs alongside the main sessions. We can’t yet accommodate diapering or infant care, so plan accordingly for younger children, and let us know your needs when you register.

The Teaching

Kids will explore a Biblical view of Creation from Genesis — a literal six-day Creation, the fall of man, the flood of Noah’s day, and restoration through the cross. We’ll also look at recent flood activity like Mount St. Helens to show similarities to Noah’s flood.

The Activities

Display and discovery tables of local fossils, dinosaur replicas, and engaging books and toys, plus scientific exploration and demonstrations — marble runs, microscopes, dinosaur digs, slime, and bubbles.
